🧠 The most important concept

RAG does NOT teach the LLM. The LLM's parameters are not changed during a normal RAG request.

Instead, RAG gives the LLM additional information at inference time.

Think of it this way:

Fine-tuning = teaching the student.
RAG = giving the student an open notebook during the exam.

RAG also doesn't necessarily reduce the number of context tokens. Instead, it avoids sending the entire knowledge base and retrieves only the most relevant pieces.
